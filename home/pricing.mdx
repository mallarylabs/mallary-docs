## Count accounts, not profiles

Each connected account, Page, or channel counts once. For example, 3 Instagram accounts and 2 Facebook Pages use 5 account spots. Multiple accounts on one platform are allowed.

Profiles group accounts for a brand or client. A profile holds one account per platform and does not add account spots. The total allowance is shared across your profiles.

Use `GET /api/v1/profiles` to read the authenticated workspace's limits. When `account_limit_scope` is `total`, use `max_accounts_total`. Existing customers keep their current terms and can have platform-based limits.

## X credits

X publishing and comments use prepaid credits at cost. Your links stay in your posts. Analytics refreshes and account lookups do not use credits.

1,000 credits = $1. Add $5, $10, $25, or $50. Credits roll over and do not expire. There are no automatic top-ups.

| X activity | Credits | Cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Post or reply without links | 15 | $0.015 |
| Post or reply with links | 200 | $0.20 |
| Billable comment resource fetched | 5 | $0.005 |
| Analytics or account lookup | 0 | Included |

Each post in a thread counts separately. A link post has one publishing charge, no matter how many links it contains. Repeat-read savings are applied before charging.

Your balance is your spending cap. If there is not enough credit, add funds in [Billing](https://mallary.ai/billing) and retry the X request. Other social platforms continue as usual. A failed request can return `insufficient_x_credits`; do not retry it in a loop without adding funds.

## Trial and billing

Start with a 14-day free subscription trial. A card is required. Cancel before it ends to avoid a subscription charge. X publishing and comments still use paid credits during the trial. Mallary has a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Existing customers keep their current prices, account limits, post allowances, and X terms. Read the workspace's returned limits and capabilities when building integrations.
